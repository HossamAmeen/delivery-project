# Store Categories and Products

## Category
A store may have multiple categories.

Examples:
- Medicines
- Groceries
- Flowers
- Personal Care
- Beverages

These are examples only; stores define their own catalog.

## Product lifecycle
ACTIVE -> INACTIVE

Availability can be separate from lifecycle so a product may remain configured
but temporarily unavailable.

## Historical snapshot
Orders preserve product information as it existed when ordered.
